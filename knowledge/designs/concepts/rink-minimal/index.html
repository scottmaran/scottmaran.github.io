How to play

Click the rink, then skate with the arrow keys or WASD.

  1. Grab the puck at center ice with Space.
  2. Press P to shoot toward the net.
  3. Body-check the computer players at speed.
  4. Press Esc to release the controls.

Game mode works best with a keyboard.
