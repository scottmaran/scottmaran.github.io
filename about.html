History

Faceoff

New Jersey.

I grew up in Jersey playing every sport imaginable. My dad introduced me to hockey and I loved it. I couldn't get enough. I watched every game, and began reading everything I could get my hands on to better understand the game.
Soon enough, one of my favorite beat writers started talking about this new things called analytics...





High School

Hockey Analytics

You know Moneyball? Hockey was going through its own analytics movement. I broke into fantasy hockey with my numbers-based approach to quantifying value and earned my own column at DobberHockey. After helping and learning from other people with their own analytics projects, I soon started my own. I published my research across my own website and as a guest on many other websites. I was even invited to present my work on special teams at the first Seattle Hockey Analtics Conference.


College

Stanford

I didn't have a specific end goal in mind when I first landed in Palo Alto, but there was no shortage of skills for me to add to my toolbox. All of the sports data collection introduced me to computer vision based automated methods. This led me to doing AI research under Itai Ashlagi and Fei-Fei Li as I studied both pure mathematical theory and low-level computer science. A math bachelors and statistics master's later, I'd stopped just studying sports and started building tools I could aim anywhere.

NFL

Working for a Team

Then football pulled me in. Every year the NFL opens up its player-tracking data through the Big Data Bowl. I used advanced machine-learning methods to dig into it, and the results got some buzz in the football-analytics world. Soon I was interning with the Arizona Cardinals — trading public data for a seat inside the building.




Sports Betting

Pricing Player Props

I figured I'd spend my career inside those buildings. Then I got put in touch with Swish Analytics, and the ground shifted from advising teams to actually having action on the line. Betting let me open up heavier statistical machinery than any front office ever needed. For nearly two years I priced player props, where being right has to survive contact with a price.

Current

Prediction markets.

Same instinct, new arena. Ask me about it.
