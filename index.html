How to play

Click the rink, then skate with the arrow keys or WASD.

  1. Grab the puck at center ice with Space.
  2. Skate to a labeled hotspot.
  3. Press Enter to open that section.
  4. Press P to shoot toward the net.

Press Esc to release the controls.

The game works best with a keyboard. The site navigation remains available above.

Inspiration Playing NHL '93 with my dad on our old Sega Genesis every day when I was home from college.
