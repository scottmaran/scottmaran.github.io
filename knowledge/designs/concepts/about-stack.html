Surface Bedrock

About

I follow things
all the way down.

Every obsession I've had started at the surface and kept pulling me deeper — until I hit the math holding it up. This is the descent.

Down the stack

01 · Surface the fan

Hockey.

It started as fandom — and a hunch that nobody was keeping the numbers I wanted. That turned into a fantasy-hockey column, writing on the Rangers, my own public analytics, and a talk at a hockey-analytics conference at 19. The questions only got harder.

02 · Deeper the data

Film & vision.

To answer them I needed better data than existed, so I spent freshman year hand-charting game film — then taught a computer to watch it for me. The first real rabbit hole: how do you turn footage into numbers?

03 · Deeper the models

Machine learning.

Computer vision was really machine learning wearing a jersey. The models under the stats quietly became the thing I actually wanted to understand.

04 · Deeper the foundation

Math.

And the models rest on math — so I majored in it. I showed up without much of a foundation, in rooms full of people who'd been at it since they were kids. It was the hardest thing I've done. I stayed until it was mine, then added a master's in statistics.

05 · Bedrock now

Same dig, new surface.

Sports → betting → prediction markets. The surface keeps changing; the move never does. I still start at the top and dig until I hit something true.
